The silver-lavender retro window represents the customizable OBS widget. The large warm gold musical note identifies the product's purpose at small sizes; the cobalt title bar and iridescent CD connect it to the Windows 95 styling and CD layout. A transparent silhouette works on light and dark desktops. No lettering is used because desktop icons must remain legible at 16–48 px.

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. The original RGBA master is preserved at `public/assets/retrotune-icon-master.png`. Format conversion produced `retrotune-icon.png` (128 px) and `retrotune.ico` with 16, 20, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128 and 256 px frames.
